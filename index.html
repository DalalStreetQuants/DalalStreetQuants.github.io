Our Recommendation: Cent Account Only

Cent accounts provide the best risk-to-capital ratio for grid trading bots. With 100 USD, you get 10,000 USC — enough margin buffer for the bot to operate safely.

Capital Requirements
10,000 INR (≈ 100 USD)
Deposit into a Cent Account → receive 10,000 USC
This is the minimum recommended capital for all DSQ bots.
USD Account Warning
A USD account needs at least 10,000 USD for safe operation. Not recommended for small capital traders.
